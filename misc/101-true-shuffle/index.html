      Bring music in

      This is the demo: signing in, importing and files are switched off here, and nothing you do is kept. Leave the demo to use your own playlists.

      Signing in is not set up yet. The page needs the client id of a Google Cloud project that the person running it creates once. It is not a secret and there is no client secret.

      1. At console.cloud.google.com, signed in with the account whose playlists you want to read, create a project and select it.
      2. APIs & Services › Library: enable YouTube Data API v3.
      3. OAuth consent screen (Google Auth Platform): audience External, publishing status Testing, yourself under Test users, and the one scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly.
      4. Credentials › Create credentials › OAuth client ID, type Web application. Authorised JavaScript origin: . Authorised redirect URI: (exactly, with the final slash).
      5. Copy the Client ID (it ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com) and paste it below, or into config.js to set it for everyone. Ignore the client secret.
      6. At the first sign-in Google says it has not verified the app. That only means the project is in Testing; the test users you listed can press Continue.

      Sign-in asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube account for one hour. The page cannot change, add or delete anything there.

          How it works. After a Google sign-in with read-only access, the page lists your YouTube playlists and Liked videos through the YouTube Data API, reads the ones you tick, and guesses artist, title and version from each video's title and channel (you can correct any of it, one track or a whole channel at a time). Genres come from the topics YouTube attaches to a video, decades from a stated release date or else the upload year. The true shuffle is a Fisher–Yates shuffle fed by the browser's cryptographic random source, kept as a bag: nothing repeats until every track in the selection has played, across reloads. The other orders are described where you choose them. Playback is YouTube's own embedded player; a video that was removed or may not be embedded is skipped and listed in the library.

          What this page stores. In this browser only (IndexedDB): the videos of the lists you imported, with the titles, channels, lengths and topics YouTube reports; your corrections, genres, tags, ratings and blocks; play counts, listening history, the queue, your listening choices and presets. The sign-in token stays in this tab's session storage for at most an hour and is revoked by Disconnect. A client id you paste is kept in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to this site, and nothing to anyone but Google (and artist names to MusicBrainz, only if you press that button). YouTube data older than 30 days is refreshed at your next sign-in; “Delete everything stored here” removes all of it; access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

          This page uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. On a phone, playback stops when the screen locks or the browser goes to the background: YouTube's player does that, and this page does not work around it. Finer genres, when asked for, are from MusicBrainz (genre data under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0). The demo's artists, tracks and playlists are invented.

How it works: every book in the Bookshelf is typed onto a 3×5 catalogue card (heading, title / statement of responsibility, imprint, physical note, subject tracings) and filed by author heading as a cataloguer would write it (Surname, Given), by title with articles dropped, by subject, by bookcase and shelf, or by year. Japanese authors are filed in a separate kana run by reading, from a small surname table; names it cannot read go under 漢字 by code point. Pencil marks and date stamps are seeded by the card's id. Fonts: Special Elite, Courier Prime and Caveat from Google Fonts; the page works without them. Nothing leaves your browser.

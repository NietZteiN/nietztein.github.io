          What the oracle knows

          Two books are "close" when the words of their catalogue descriptions, titles and authors overlap, weighted so that rare words count for more than common ones (TF-IDF cosine), with a small nudge for sharing a genre and a language. It is not a reading of the books: two novels about the same thing in different words are strangers to it, and because the descriptions are a sentence or two long, a single shared surname or place name can carry a whole connection. The route is a greedy nearest-neighbour walk tidied by 2-opt, so it is a good path, not the best one, and it changes with the start you choose.

          Reading the map

          Stations are books in route order, spiralling out from the start. Colour is genre; the colour switches halfway between two stations and the ringed station begins the new line. Hover a station for the words that tie it to the previous stop; zoom in for titles.

          Data

          The site's own library catalogue (assets/data/library.json: titles, authors, genres and short descriptions of the books on Jack's shelves). Nothing is fetched from anywhere else; everything is computed in your browser. If the catalogue cannot be loaded, a small set of invented books stands in.

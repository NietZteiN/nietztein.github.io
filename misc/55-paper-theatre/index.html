    A kamishibai (紙芝居) is a street theatre of paper boards slid through a wooden frame. Here every board is a paper or a blog post from this site, told as a short visual novel: a hook, a question, a bet you place, the result as the source printed it, and a citation card. Figures marked § are quoted from the source; dialogue is dramatized.

    Stories are plain-text .vn scripts in stories/; the engine is one dependency-free file. Progress and preferences are kept in your browser. Nothing leaves your browser.

    Backlog

      Paper Theatre

      Pick a story. Each line types itself out; press once to finish the line, again to go on. When a question comes up, choose with the buttons or the number keys. Choices change reactions, never the reported figures. The end card has the citation, the figures used and where to read the source.

      Keys

      SpaceEnter→advance (first press completes the line); hold Space to skip ←Backspaceback one line 1–9choose an option Aauto-advance (pauses on choices) Sskip seen text; Shift+S skips unseen text too Lbacklog, with “rewind here” Ttext-only transcript Dlight / dark Rrestart the story H or ?this help Escclose a panel

      Touch

      Tap the stage to advance, swipe left to advance and right to go back, long-press to toggle auto, tap the name tag for the backlog.

      Links

      ?story=id, &at=label for a moment, ?post=slug to auto-read a blog post, ?src=stories/draft.vn to preview a script, ?drafts=1 to list drafts, ?theme=dark, ?cast=1 for the cast sheet.

      Your place in each story and your speed setting are saved in this browser only.

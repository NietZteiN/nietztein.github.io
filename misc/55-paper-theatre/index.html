    A kamishibai (紙芝居) is a street theatre of paper boards slid through a wooden frame. Here every board is a paper or a blog post from this site, told as a short visual novel: a hook, a question, a bet you place, the result as the source printed it, and a citation card. Figures marked § are quoted from the source; dialogue is dramatized.

    Stories are plain-text .vn scripts in stories/; the engine and all of its art are code, with no images. Progress, saves and preferences are kept in your browser. Nothing leaves your browser.

    Paper Theatre

    Pick a story, then Start. Each line types itself out; press once to finish the line, again to go on. When a question comes up, choose with the bars or the number keys. Choices change reactions, never the reported figures. The ending has the citation, the figures used and where to read the source.

    Touch

    Tap the stage to advance, swipe left to advance and right to go back, long-press to toggle auto, tap the name plate for the backlog. The quick menu sits at the bottom right of the text window.

    Links

    ?story=id, &at=label for a moment, ?post=slug to auto-read a blog post, ?src=stories/draft.vn to preview a script, ?drafts=1 to list drafts, ?theme=dark, ?cast=1 for the cast sheet, ?gallery=bg or ?gallery=cg for the scenery.

    Your place in each story, six save slots per story and your settings are saved in this browser only.

Paper Theatre: sound check

Renders every track and every ambience of the score offline (OfflineAudioContext, the same graph the stage plays), measures it and draws a spectrogram of each render: time across, frequency up on a log scale from 40 Hz to 11 kHz, the dashed line at 8 kHz. Below those: each effect, the two stings, the chord a menu hangs on and a run of voice blips, rendered alone. ?story=<id> picks the story whose theme is used; ?bars=N the length; ?manual=1 skips the measuring and leaves only the buttons, which play everything live after a click.

A software raycaster in the Wolfenstein 3D manner, drawn pixel by pixel on a 2D canvas at half resolution. The level is built from Jack's library catalogue: each bookcase unit becomes an aisle, each shelf a stretch of wall, and the wall texture is the shelf's actual row of spines, generated at load time from title, author, genre and page-ish heuristics. Data: assets/data/library.json (889 books). No libraries, nothing leaves the browser.

You are standing in a hall. Thirteen aisles open off it, one per bookcase unit, and the ends of the aisles are signposted. Walk down one and the books on either side are the real ones, in shelf order. Look straight at a spine and pull it to read the catalogue entry.

Shelves are laid out from the u/s/p fields of the catalogue (unit, shelf, position). Spine width comes from the type of book and the length of its description, colour from a hash of the genre, and a few get foil. The cat is real but lives elsewhere.

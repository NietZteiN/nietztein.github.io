An isometric model of the room built from the catalogue: each bookcase is drawn at its real shelf count with its real books in left-to-right order, coloured by genre, and the 53 things that are not books (CDs, visual-novel boxes, notebooks, a headset, trophies, a board game) stand where they really stand. The floor plan is a guess until the owner arranges it. Data: assets/data/library.json (889 books); no libraries, nothing leaves the browser.

Reading Room

Twelve bookcases, a desk, and 942 things on the shelves, each where the catalogue says it is. Walk up to a case and press E (or click it) to see the shelf close up; click a spine for the book.

Arrange turns the room into a floor-plan editor: drag a piece to a tile, R rotates it, overlaps show red, Ctrl+Z undoes. The plan is saved in this browser; export layout JSON writes the same shape as the default in layout.js, so the real room can replace the guess.

Spines come from the u/s/p fields of the catalogue (unit, shelf, position); widths and heights follow the kind of book. Props are drawn from words in the object's catalogue entry. The cat is also a guess.

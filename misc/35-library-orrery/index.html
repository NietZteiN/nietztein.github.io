            Each book's orbital period grows with its age (T = 0.25 + 0.016 · age1.5 years) and its distance follows from Kepler's third law, so this year's books whizz round in months while Gilgamesh has not yet finished a single lap. An author with several books becomes a moon system on a shared orbit. Undated books drift in the Oort cloud. Phases are fixed at 2000.0, so everyone sees the same sky at the same moment.

    Blog Post Speedrun

    This page renders the site's own blog posts with a LiveSplit-style timer beside them: it starts on your first scroll and stops once the end-of-post marker has been on screen for one second. Splits are the post's headings, each shown against your personal best and a fake world record. Everything is stored in this browser only and none of it matters, which is the point.

    • Any%: anything goes. 100%: every post back to back, one segment per post. Glitchless: never scroll faster than 2500 px/s or the run is rejected. Blindfolded: the text is blurred and you scroll by feel.
    • Splits fire when a heading passes the top of the window. Posts with fewer than three headings use the 25%, 50% and 75% marks instead.
    • Anti-cheat theatre: Ctrl+End, or one jump of more than 60% of the page, marks the run as TAS (tool-assisted). It goes on the board with a label but never counts as a personal best.
    • World records are computed, not run: words ÷ 900 wpm + 1.5 s with seeded jitter, credited to a generated runner. They are labelled unverified because they are fictional. "Human" is the same post at 250 wpm.
    • Leaderboards keep the top ten per post and category in localStorage. Reset clears one board. Nothing is sent anywhere.
    • Rendering uses marked from jsDelivr, with a small built-in renderer as fallback. Links in posts open in a new tab so the run survives.
    • Hash state: #/any/<slug>, #/glitchless/<slug>, #/blind/<slug>, #/all.

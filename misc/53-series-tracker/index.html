Reading the catalogue…

Series names and volume numbers are read off the catalogue titles ("Vol. 3", "第3巻", "(3)", "③", "III", "上/下", a bare trailing number) and grouped after folding width, case and accents, with author and publisher as tie-breakers and a short hand-written override list. The library cannot know how long a series really is, so "of n" always means the highest volume seen here, never the true length. Data: assets/data/library.json. No libraries, nothing leaves the browser.

Runs

A manga-shop view of one real library. Each row is a series: the numbered spines are the volumes that are physically on the shelves, the dashed ones are the numbers in between that are not. Click a spine for the book and a link to its card on the Bookshelf; click a series name for where its volumes live, which shelves they are split across, and the years they span.

"6 of 7" means six volumes are here and the highest one seen is 7. Nothing here knows how long a series really is, so a run with no gaps is only complete as far as it goes.

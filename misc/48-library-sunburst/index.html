The whole library as one ring. Genres inside, 889 books on the rim; click a wedge to dive in. ← Bookshelf

Every leaf is one real book from the catalogue, so the rim is 889 thin arcs and each inner ring is sized by count; colours are the site's genre hues, lightening toward the rim. Click a wedge to zoom, the centre (or Esc) to go back up, and hover for the books on that shelf. The partition, squarified treemap and icicle layouts are drawn straight onto SVG with no libraries. Data: Jack's library catalogue (library.json).

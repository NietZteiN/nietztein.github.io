How it works. The visited list is one constant at the top of app.js. Country shapes are Natural Earth 1:110m via world-atlas (ISC), drawn with d3 and topojson-client; land share is the spherical area of the visited polygons over all land on that geometry; population shares are mid-2024 estimates from the UN World Population Prospects 2024; the night shadow and the sun row use a plain solar-position formula. There is a drop-in version of this map for any page: widget.js. Nothing leaves your browser.

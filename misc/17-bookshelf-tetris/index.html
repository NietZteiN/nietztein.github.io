The alphabet rule

The pine bookcase is alphabetized by title, ignoring case and accents. One convention is special: titles beginning with "The" are grouped after S and before T, so "The Trial" sits after "Sein und Zeit" and before "Tristram Shandy". "A" and "An" are not moved, so "A History of Japan" files under A. Titles in non-Latin scripts, such as Japanese, come after all Latin titles.

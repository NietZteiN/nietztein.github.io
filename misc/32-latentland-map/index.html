How to read this map

Each hex is a place, caste, school or procedure from Latentland, the memoir of a single neuron; you begin at Unit 4091 with 64 tokens of surprise and pay a hex's terrain cost to enter it, less when the model already has context (two or more explored neighbours) and more when it is isolated. When the surprise runs out the context window closes, unless you switch on free roam, which costs nothing and is for reading; caches of surprise wait at a few places, and reading a chronicle (a blog post linked from a hex) restores 8 tokens once per post. The red dashed line is the story's own route, which Next in the story follows one beat per click, with a line from the book at each step.

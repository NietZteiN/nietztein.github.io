About this map

Every place on the map is a concept from Latentland: A Romance of One Dimension, the memoir of Unit 4091, a single neuron in the eleventh layer of a language model. The quotes are lines from the book with their page numbers; the descriptions paraphrase it in the Unit's voice; each gloss names the real idea in plain English. Read the source: the PDF and the blog post.

Story follows the memoir's route across 42 places, revealing the map as it goes. Expedition is a game on the same map: each step costs tokens of surprise, the way a model's context window pays for what it did not predict, and the window holds 64.

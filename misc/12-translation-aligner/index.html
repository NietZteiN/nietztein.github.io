The nudge is a heuristic, not a dictionary. A word counts as reflected if the glossary maps it to an English word found in the aligned target, if it is a number that appears in the target, or if its romaji loosely resembles an English word there (katakana and kana words only). Everything else is only "possibly dropped". Click a highlighted word to mark it as covered (saved in the alignment file); shift-click to add it to the glossary.
The sample poem is the opening of "Yogoretchimatta kanashimi ni" by Nakahara Chuya (1934, public domain in Japan; he died in 1937). The English beside it is a rough literal gloss written for this demo, not a published translation.

    How it works. Every star is an author of the catalogue (aliases merged), sized by how many of their books are in the house and coloured by their commonest genre. Lines join authors who share a shelf, bookcase, genre, publisher, language or decade; only each author's strongest few are drawn, and the nebulae are label-propagation clusters. Hover a star for its books, click to pin it and read why its neighbours are neighbours, drag to move, double-click to let go. Data: the library catalogue.

    Each post is one continuous spiral groove: every letter adds its own wiggle (a stable amplitude and frequency per character), spaces run flat, sentence ends are short gaps and paragraphs are the shiny track gaps. The tonearm reads the sentence under the stylus aloud with your browser's own voices; click the record to drop the needle, drag it backwards to scratch.

    Text from this site's blog (blog/index.json). Speech, noise and the PNG export all happen in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

    Phonograph

    Pick a post and press play. The record spins at 33⅓ (times the speed you set), the strobe dots on the platter rim stand still only at true speed, and the VU needles jump with the length of each word. Sentences are spoken with the voices your browser ships with; if it has none, the words still light up on the display.

    Click anywhere on the record to drop the needle at that depth of the post. Drag the record backwards to scratch. Flip the record for side B: the second half of the post, or, when the post is too short to split, a remix of its words sorted by frequency. The sleeve art is drawn from the post's tags and slug, so each post has its own cover.

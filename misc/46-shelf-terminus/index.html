Genre lines

A genre gets a line where it has three or more books on a unit. Click a genre to isolate it; hover a station for its books.

A transit puzzle in the manner of Mini Metro, drawn from one real library: the thirteen stations are the bookcases in Jack's catalogue, sized by how many books they hold, and every passenger is an actual book that wants to get back to the unit where it is shelved. Books board in a seeded order that changes daily, so a day's timetable can be replayed. The service map drops the game and draws where each genre really lives. Data: assets/data/library.json (889 books). No libraries; nothing leaves the browser.

Terminus

Drag from one station to another to open a line (up to six). Trains shuttle along each line, carrying six books at a time, and books change trains at shared stations to reach the bookcase where they live. Drag a line's end tab to extend it, drag a segment onto a station to insert a stop, or use the chips in the sidebar to drop stops and delete lines.

A station with more than eight books waiting starts to overcrowd; if the red ring closes, the service is suspended. Every Sunday night you pick an upgrade: another train, a tunnel under the river that divides the Japanese cases from the English ones, a bigger carriage, or an interchange that holds fourteen books. New line colours unlock weekly.

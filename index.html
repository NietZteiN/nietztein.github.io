Undergraduate researcher at the University of Texas at Dallas since August 2022, with Phi Kappa Phi and Collegium V Honors. His research centers on language models — how they represent meaning, where they generalize, and where they break — with broader interests connecting machine learning to questions about cognition and scientific discovery. He has held a DAAD RISE research internship in Germany and a policy-oriented internship at CSET, alongside applied work in computer vision, software engineering, and ML for materials and scientific text. He teaches and grades introductory CS courses and is active in student organizations and outreach.

Research Interests

  • Language models and the structure of meaning they learn — including representation analysis, generalization, and behavioral evaluation of frontier systems.
  • Connections between large language models and human language processing, drawing on tools from computational linguistics and cognitive science.
  • Adjacent interests include machine learning for scientific and patent text, ML for materials science, and fairness and representation evaluation in high-stakes AI systems.

My library

Small things built for fun. Each one is a plain web page with no accounts, no servers and no tracking beyond the site's own. They open in a new tab. Source for the older ones is in NietZteiN/side-projects; the newest live in this site's own repository.

Shelf Genome

The library as one chromosome: genre bands, year glow, collision hotspots, and fake crossover books.
Read-Order Oracle

A subway map through all 889 books, each stop close to the last by shared words; ask it what to read next.
Book Exchange

Every library book is a listed stock; prices move when its words show up on the blog. Paper-trade 10,000 credits.
Grammar Fossil Dig

Paste English and dig down through its word strata: modern loans, Latin, Norman French, Norse, Old English bedrock.
Homophone Bridge

Cross a river of six languages on planks that sound alike; score for how far the meanings drift.
Pidgin Generator

Collide two languages into a toy pidgin: lexicon, phonology, grammar sketch, phrasebook and corpus, all live.
Dead Authors' Local Time

A world clock for the places where the authors on the shelves died, sorted by whose sun is setting right now.
Palimpsest

A vellum leaf that remembers your hand: where you rub, an older hidden text shows through, across visits.
Site Reverse Engineer

The site's own JavaScript, obfuscated live with the Playground's transforms, plus a daily guessing game.
Blog Post Speedrun

Speedrun the blog: a LiveSplit-style timer, splits, fake unverified world records, a local leaderboard and TAS detection.
Latentland Map

Latentland as an explorable hex map: every hex a concept from the story, every step costs surprise, fog lifts as you read.
Ghost Commit Player

Any public GitHub repo replayed as a silent film: intertitles for commits, the file tree growing between.
Zipf Karaoke

Words fade by how common they are, then get sung: common ones vanish in a beat, rare ones ring and linger.
Tokenizer Aquarium

Live BPE tokenisation as a fish tank: common tokens swim big and slow, rare ones small and twitchy.
Library Roguelike

A daily text roguelike where every room is a real shelf of the library and every monster is a book.
Tide Table for Prose

A clock whose face is a paragraph: one word turns into a synonym every minute until midnight.
Haiku clock

The current time, told as a haiku in four languages.
Daylight clock

Local time, sunrise, sunset and daylight arcs for three cities at once.
Bookshelf Tetris

Falling pieces are books. A shelf only clears when its titles read in alphabetical order.
Virtual library

A library catalog as shelves of spines: find a book, browse stats, run integrity checks.

Runs on an invented sample catalog.
Bookshelf poster

A whole book collection as one printable SVG poster of spines, sized to the paper you pick.
Obfuscation playground

Stack JavaScript obfuscation transforms into a ladder and diff each rung against the last.
Obfuscation game

Predict the output of obfuscated Python snippets. Tracks correctness, time and confidence.
Grokking microscope

A one-layer transformer learning addition mod 113. Scrub through training and watch it grok.
Git film timeline

A git history laid out like a video editor, with a trailer that plays the whole thing in 30 seconds.

Shows a bundled example repository.
Harvard Classics oracle

Ask a question or name a mood and get a passage from the Five-Foot Shelf. Also today's Fifteen Minutes a Day reading.

Searches a sample of 340 passages from seven works.
Poetry form checker

Paste a poem and get morae, syllables, meter and rhyme annotated line by line.
Translation aligner

A source text and its translation side by side, sentence by sentence, with manual realignment.
Aozora frequency reader

Sakaguchi Ango's 堕落論 with the words he uses unusually often highlighted against general Japanese.
Fake paper titles

A Markov chain that invents ML paper titles, and a linter that scores how cliché a real one is.
